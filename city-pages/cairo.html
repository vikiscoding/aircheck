    Where these numbers come from

    US AQI and UV index come from the Open-Meteo air-quality API (CAMS global), hour by hour. Feels-like highs come from the Open-Meteo historical weather API, day by day. Each value is the median across 2023–2025, retrieved 1 Nov 2026. CAMS is a model on a grid tens of kilometres wide. It describes the wider city area, not one street, and it can differ from local monitoring stations. Its US AQI averages particles over a day, so a single hour is smoothed. For a health decision, check a local official source.
