    How a reading works

    The sentence comes from a fixed table written ahead of time. Nothing on this page asks a model to describe the weather.

    1. You give a place, or you allow a one-time location reading.
    2. The page asks Open-Meteo for the current weather and air quality.
    3. It turns those readings into three bands and looks up a sentence in the table.
    4. You see the sentence, the numbers, the time, and the source.

    Privacy

    No account. No history. If you use your location, the coordinates are used once for the request and then discarded. This page does not use cookies, local storage, or analytics.
